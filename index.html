Explainable AI Risk Intelligence Engine

Deterministic Heuristic Model v2.1: Multi-factor scoring breakdown with mathematical weights.

Formula Architecture & Weights

Risk Score = min(100.0, max(5.0, Base Hazard + Urgency + Population + Vulnerabilities + Weather Amplification))
  • Baseline Hazards: Earthquake (45 pts), Landslide (40 pts), Flood (35 pts), Fire (35 pts), Severe Weather (25 pts).
  • Reported Severity: Minor (+5 pts), Moderate (+15 pts), Severe (+28 pts), Catastrophic (+40 pts).
  • Civilian Exposure: 1-5 (+8 pts), 6-20 (+16 pts), 21-50 (+24 pts), >50 (+30 pts).
  • Vulnerability Factors: Trapped (+15 pts), Oxygen / Medical (+12 pts), Mobility Impaired (+9 pts), Elderly (+7 pts), Children (+6 pts). Capped at +25 pts.
  • Environmental Amplification: Rainfall ≥ 15 mm/hr (+10 to +15 pts for floods), Wind ≥ 40 km/h (+14 pts for fire/storm).

API Integration Registry & Attributions

  • Open-Meteo: Weather telemetry provided by Open-Meteo.com under CC BY 4.0. Forecasts represent model projections, not confirmed ground disasters.
  • USGS Earthquake Hazards Program: GeoJSON feeds provided by the United States Geological Survey. Events represent measured seismic tremors, not proof of casualty or structural collapse.
  • NDMA SACHET: Public CAP/RSS feed by the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), Government of India.
  • NASA FIRMS: Active fire satellite detections provided by NASA ESDIS FIRMS. Thermal anomalies represent orbital sensor readings, not ground fires.
  • OpenStreetMap & Leaflet: Cartography © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • OSRM Routing: Route directions calculated via OSRM. Directions are advisory only; physical field passability takes precedence.
